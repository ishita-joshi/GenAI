# Staff Engineer Interview Loop

The Staff Engineer loop consists of 6 rounds, scheduled across 2-3 weeks for external candidates. Total candidate time investment: ~7 hours of interviews plus ~4-6 hours of preparation.

## The Six Rounds

### 1. Recruiter Screen (30 min)
Conducted by a recruiter. Covers background, motivation, comp expectations (early signal only — do not commit), and timeline. Filters for obvious non-fits.

### 2. Hiring Manager Screen (45 min)
Conducted by the hiring manager. Covers candidate's most recent technical projects in depth, scope of impact, and reasoning for the role change. This is the most important early signal for whether the candidate calibrates to Staff vs Senior.

### 3. Technical Deep Dive (60 min)
Conducted by a Staff or Senior Staff engineer on the hiring team. Candidate walks through a past project of their choice. The interviewer probes for technical depth, tradeoff reasoning, and what the candidate would do differently. No coding in this round.

### 4. System Design (90 min)
Conducted by two engineers (one from the hiring team, one from a partner team). Open-ended design problem appropriate to the team — e.g., for platform candidates, "design a multi-tenant feature flag service that supports 100k QPS with sub-10ms latency." Looks for scope decomposition, tradeoff articulation, and how the candidate handles ambiguity.

### 5. Coding (60 min)
Conducted by a Senior or Staff engineer. Medium-complexity problem, usually involves API design or refactoring rather than algorithms. Staff candidates are evaluated more on code quality, testability, and how they explain decisions than on raw speed.

### 6. Cross-Functional / Values (45 min)
Conducted by a non-engineering partner (PM, EM from another team, or designer). Probes for cross-functional communication, handling of disagreement, and alignment with engineering values.

## Bar Raiser

In addition to the six standard rounds, every Staff loop includes a bar raiser — a Staff or Senior Staff engineer from *outside* the hiring team who reviews the full debrief packet and votes independently. The bar raiser has veto power; if the bar raiser votes no-hire, the offer does not go out regardless of how the hiring team voted.

## Scheduling Notes

For external candidates, all 6 rounds should be scheduled within a 2-week window. Longer windows are the single biggest cause of strong candidates dropping out. The recruiter should batch rounds 2-3 into one day and rounds 4-6 into another.
