# Interview Scorecard Rubric

Every interviewer submits a scorecard in Greenhouse within 24 hours of the interview. The scorecard has two parts: a structured rating per competency axis (1-5) and a written summary.

## Rating Scale

- **Strong Hire (5)**: I would actively recruit this person to my team.
- **Hire (4)**: I would be happy to work with this person.
- **Lean Hire (3)**: I have minor concerns but they are addressable.
- **Lean No-Hire (2)**: I have significant concerns; would need very strong signal elsewhere.
- **No Hire (1)**: I would not work with this person.

There is no "neutral" rating. Interviewers must commit.

## Per-Competency Ratings

Each interview targets 1-2 of the 5 competency axes from the leveling rubric. The interviewer rates the candidate on those axes specifically and writes evidence. Generic comments like "seemed strong" are not acceptable evidence.

## Debrief Format

The debrief is run by the hiring manager 24-48 hours after the loop. Order of speaking: interviewers from lowest-rating to highest. This prevents the highest-rating interviewer from anchoring the room.

The bar raiser speaks last and may overrule the room.

## Common Calibration Errors

**Anchoring to the recruiter's pitch.** The recruiter may have pre-pitched the candidate at Staff; the interviewer must evaluate against the rubric, not the pitch.

**Confusing "smart" with "Staff."** Many Senior engineers are smart. Staff requires evidence of cross-team impact, not just intelligence.

**Using "I would hire them" instead of "I would hire them at this level."** The scorecard rating is level-specific. A Strong Hire for Senior is not a Strong Hire for Staff.
