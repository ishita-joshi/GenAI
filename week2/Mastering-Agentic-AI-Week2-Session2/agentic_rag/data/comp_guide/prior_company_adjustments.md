# Prior Company Adjustments

The "prior company" adjustment governs how we calibrate offers relative to the candidate's previous employer. The intent is fairness, not parity — we are not matching the prior offer, we are pricing the level the candidate calibrates to here.

## Tier Definitions

**Tier 1 (FAANG and peer):** Google, Meta, Apple, Amazon, Microsoft, Netflix, Stripe, Databricks, OpenAI, Anthropic. Candidates from these companies typically have strong signal on technical depth and can be assumed competitive in coding and system design.

**Tier 2:** Mid-stage scaleups with strong engineering brand: Airbnb, Snowflake, Datadog, Figma, Notion, Linear, Vercel.

**Tier 3:** Everyone else, including early-stage startups, mid-cap public companies, and consulting firms.

## Level Mapping from FAANG

This is the most common mapping question. Use it as a starting hypothesis, not a guarantee — the interview loop is still the source of truth.

| FAANG Title | Typical Our Level |
|---|---|
| Software Engineer II (Google L4) | L3 Senior |
| Senior Software Engineer (Google L5) | L4 Senior II |
| Staff Software Engineer (Google L6) | **L5 Staff** |
| Senior Staff (Google L7) | L6 Senior Staff |
| Principal (Google L8) | L7 Principal |

Years of experience is a secondary signal. A FAANG candidate with 9 years of experience and a Staff title at their current company should be evaluated against our L5 Staff rubric. If they pass, they should be offered toward the high end of the L5 band (top quartile) because they have demonstrably operated at Staff scope at a peer company.

## Comp Premiums

We do not pay a "FAANG premium" beyond what is already reflected in the band. The L5 band is set with FAANG candidates as the calibration point. Tier 2 candidates may land in the same band; Tier 3 candidates calibrated to L5 typically land in the lower half of the band.

## YOE Heuristics for Staff

- 6-8 YOE Staff candidate from Tier 1: midpoint of L5 band is the default offer
- 9-12 YOE Staff candidate from Tier 1: top quartile of L5 band, VP sign-off pre-cleared
- 12+ YOE candidate still at Staff (not Senior Staff): flag for re-calibration; long tenure at the same level may indicate a scope gap
