# Signing Bonus Policy

Signing bonuses are used to bridge the gap when a candidate is leaving unvested equity at their current employer. They are not a general negotiation tool.

## Standard Ranges

| Level | Standard Signing | Maximum (with VP approval) |
|---|---|---|
| L3 | $10,000 | $25,000 |
| L4 | $20,000 | $50,000 |
| **L5 Staff** | **$40,000** | **$100,000** |
| L6 | $75,000 | $200,000 |
| L7 | $150,000 | $400,000 |

## Unvested Equity Bridge

For Staff candidates leaving substantial unvested equity at a Tier 1 employer, the signing bonus may be calculated as 25-40% of the unvested equity value, capped at the maximum above. The candidate must provide written documentation of the unvested grant; verbal claims are not sufficient.

## Clawback

Signing bonuses are clawed back 100% if the employee leaves within 12 months, 50% within 24 months. This is disclosed in the offer letter and the candidate must acknowledge it.

## FAANG-Specific Notes

FAANG candidates typically have refresher grants stacked on top of new-hire grants, so their unvested equity in years 2-4 is often higher than the initial grant suggests. Request a screenshot of the full vesting schedule, not just the original offer letter.
