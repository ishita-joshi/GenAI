# Compensation Bands — 2025

All bands are for the US, location-adjusted for SF/NYC tier 1 metro areas. Adjust down 5-10% for tier 2 metros and 10-15% for tier 3. International bands are maintained separately.

Equity is 4-year vest with 1-year cliff, quarterly thereafter. Equity values shown are total grant value at offer.

## IC Bands (Base / Equity / Target Bonus)

| Level | Base ($) | Equity ($, 4yr) | Target Bonus |
|---|---|---|---|
| L3 Senior | 180,000 – 220,000 | 200,000 – 320,000 | 10% |
| L4 Senior II | 210,000 – 250,000 | 320,000 – 480,000 | 12% |
| **L5 Staff** | **250,000 – 310,000** | **480,000 – 720,000** | **15%** |
| L6 Senior Staff | 300,000 – 370,000 | 720,000 – 1,100,000 | 18% |
| L7 Principal | 360,000 – 450,000 | 1,100,000 – 1,800,000 | 20% |

## L5 Staff Detail

For a Staff Engineer offer, the typical total comp (base + equity/4 + bonus) lands between $430,000 and $580,000 per year. The high end of the band is reserved for candidates with:
- 8+ years of relevant experience
- Strong prior signal from a FAANG or peer company (Tier 1)
- Multiple competing offers (subject to comp committee approval)

## Approval Authority

- **Within band, below midpoint:** no committee approval needed (hiring manager + recruiter).
- **Within band, above midpoint:** VP Engineering sign-off required.
- **Above band:** comp committee (CFO + VP Eng + CEO).

Hiring managers must check the band before extending a verbal offer. Verbal offers above midpoint without VP sign-off are the single most common cause of escalations.

## Out-of-Band Exceptions

We approve approximately 4-6 out-of-band offers per year, almost exclusively at L6+. Out-of-band approvals at L5 are rare and require demonstrated competing offer documentation.
