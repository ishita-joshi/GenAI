# Staff Engineer (L5) — Level Expectations

The Staff Engineer level is the first level at which technical impact is expected to extend meaningfully beyond a single team. Staff engineers are senior individual contributors who set technical direction for multiple teams or a critical system, and who consistently deliver work that others depend on.

## Scope of Impact

A Staff Engineer's work affects multiple teams or a major system over a 6–12 month horizon. They are responsible for the technical health of a domain (e.g., the platform's data layer, the inference pipeline, the developer experience tooling) — not just the projects they personally write code for.

## Core Competencies

**Technical depth.** Recognized expert in at least one technical area. Staff engineers are the person other senior engineers ask when they're stuck. They write the canonical design doc for their domain.

**Cross-team influence.** Drives technical alignment across 2+ teams. Identifies and resolves technical disagreements between teams without escalating to management. Writes RFCs that other teams adopt voluntarily.

**Ambiguity tolerance.** Takes problems that arrive as "something is wrong with X" and decomposes them into a concrete plan. Owns the decomposition, not just the execution.

**Mentorship.** Actively levels up 2-3 senior engineers per year. Reviews their designs, gives concrete feedback, advocates for their promotions.

**Communication.** Writes design docs that engineering managers send to other teams as exemplars. Presents at engineering all-hands. Translates technical risk into business terms for non-engineering stakeholders.

## What Staff Is Not

Staff is not "Senior but with more years." A Senior engineer (L4) who has been senior for 5 years is not automatically a Staff engineer. The differentiating signal is scope: Staff candidates have demonstrably affected work outside their own team, with evidence we can point to.

Staff is also not a management track. Staff engineers are individual contributors. The management equivalent at this level is Engineering Manager.

## Platform-Specific Staff Expectations

For Staff engineers on the platform team specifically, the expectation is technical ownership of a multi-tenant system used by 3+ consumer teams. Staff platform engineers are evaluated on developer-experience metrics (time-to-first-API-call, on-call burden imposed on consumer teams) in addition to raw system performance.
