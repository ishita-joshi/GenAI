# IC Engineering Levels — Overview

We maintain seven IC levels, mapped to titles as follows:

| Level | Title | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | Engineer I | Tasks within a sprint, with guidance |
| L2 | Engineer II | Features within a quarter, mostly autonomous |
| L3 | Senior Engineer | Projects spanning a quarter, fully autonomous |
| L4 | Senior Engineer II | Multi-quarter projects, leads small initiatives |
| L5 | Staff Engineer | Multi-team or system-level technical leadership |
| L6 | Senior Staff Engineer | Org-level technical strategy |
| L7 | Principal Engineer | Company-wide technical direction |

## Scope Progression

The single most important dimension that separates levels is *scope of impact* and the time horizon over which that impact is judged.

- L3 (Senior): single team, single quarter
- L4 (Senior II): single team, 2-3 quarters
- L5 (Staff): 2+ teams or a critical system, 6-12 months
- L6 (Senior Staff): engineering org, 12-18 months
- L7 (Principal): company, 18+ months

## Calibration Notes

Calibrating between L4 and L5 is the most common point of disagreement in hiring loops. The bright line: does the candidate have evidence of *technical leadership outside their own team*, or only inside it?

External candidates from FAANG companies often have titles that don't map 1:1. A "Senior Software Engineer" at Google (Google L5) is usually mapped to L4 here. A "Staff Software Engineer" at Google (Google L6) usually maps to our L5. See the Prior Company Adjustments doc in the comp guide for the full mapping table.
