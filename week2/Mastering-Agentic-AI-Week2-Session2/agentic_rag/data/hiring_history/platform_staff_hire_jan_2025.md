# Hire Debrief: Platform Staff, January 2025

Candidate: Senior Software Engineer at Meta (Meta E5), 8 years of experience, joined as Staff Engineer on Platform.

## Calibration

The candidate was originally pitched by the recruiter as a Senior II (L4) candidate based on title parity. The hiring manager flagged in the screen that the candidate's actual scope at Meta — they had owned the company's internal feature flag rollout system used by 12 product teams — was Staff scope at our company.

The loop calibrated the candidate to L5 Staff. Bar raiser concurred. This is a useful precedent: title-to-level parity from Meta does not work directly because Meta's E5 is broader in scope expectation than our L4.

## Offer

- Base: $295,000 (high end of L5 band)
- Equity: $640,000 / 4yr
- Signing: $80,000 (unvested Meta equity bridge, ~30% of unvested grant)
- Total comp year 1: ~$535,000

The signing required VP approval. Approved on the basis of unvested equity documentation; not a precedent for offers without documented unvested grants.

## Lessons

1. Recruiter pitches based on title parity are unreliable for FAANG candidates. The hiring manager screen must do real scope calibration.
2. The candidate dropped from a competing offer at Stripe after we moved their second-round interviews up by a week. Speed of process matters more than band positioning at this level.
3. The bar raiser was from the consumer-facing API team — they were the right choice because they had recently consumed feature flag work and could probe for customer empathy.
