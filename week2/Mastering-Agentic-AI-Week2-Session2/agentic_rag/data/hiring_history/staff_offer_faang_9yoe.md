# Hire Debrief: Staff Hire, FAANG, 9 YOE — April 2025

Candidate: Staff Software Engineer at Google (Google L6), 9 years of total experience, hired into Platform team as L5 Staff. Highly relevant precedent for the current Platform Staff opening.

## Calibration

Direct level mapping: Google L6 → our L5 Staff. The candidate had operated at Staff scope at Google for 2.5 years prior; the interview loop confirmed all five rubric axes at 5/5.

The candidate's strongest signal was on Technical Scope: they had owned the migration of Google's internal experimentation framework from one storage backend to another, coordinating with 7 consumer teams over 11 months. The platform team's hiring manager specifically called this out as the "exactly what we want" precedent.

## Offer

- Base: $305,000 (top of L5 band — required VP sign-off because it crossed midpoint by a wide margin)
- Equity: $700,000 / 4yr (high end of band)
- Signing: $95,000 (Google unvested equity bridge)
- Target bonus: 15%
- Total comp year 1: ~$580,000

VP sign-off was obtained before the verbal extension. The recruiter held the verbal for 36 hours while the comp committee reviewed the equity grant. The candidate accepted within 4 hours of the written offer.

## Lessons

1. For FAANG Staff candidates with 8+ YOE, top-of-band is the realistic offer, not midpoint. Pre-clear with VP before the loop closes, not after.
2. The candidate had a competing verbal from Stripe. Speed of written offer was the deciding factor — Stripe's offer came 3 days later.
3. This candidate is the calibration precedent for the next Platform Staff hire. Bar raiser explicitly called it out: "this is the bar."
