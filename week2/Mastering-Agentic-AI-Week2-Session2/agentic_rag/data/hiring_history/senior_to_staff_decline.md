# Hire Debrief: Staff Decline, March 2025

Candidate: Staff Engineer at a Tier 2 scaleup, 11 years of experience, applied for Platform Staff.

## Outcome

Decline at the loop stage. Recommended for re-engagement at Senior II (L4) in 12-18 months if scope expanded.

## Calibration Signal

The candidate scored 5/5 on Technical Depth, 5/5 on Communication, but 3/5 on Technical Scope and 3/5 on Influence & Leadership.

In the deep dive, the candidate could not point to a project that affected teams outside their own. Their largest project was a major rewrite of their team's data pipeline — high technical complexity, but no cross-team scope. The bar raiser noted: "smart, would hire at L4, not at L5."

This is the most common Staff decline pattern at our company. Candidates with long tenure at scaleups often have strong depth but limited cross-team scope because their org was smaller. The rubric is unambiguous: technical scope under 4 is not Staff, regardless of YOE.

## Lessons

1. Years of experience is the least useful signal at the Staff level. An 11 YOE candidate calibrated below an 8 YOE candidate from a larger org because scope is dominant.
2. The "minimum score" rubric rule was critical here — by average, the candidate would have scored 4.2/5, which on a permissive read could be Staff. The rubric explicitly prevents this.
3. The candidate was disappointed but not surprised by the feedback. Honest feedback in the rejection improved the candidate's view of our process — they recommended a peer to us six weeks later.
